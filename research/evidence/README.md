# Evidence registry

No active species rule is included yet. The first MVP candidate is *Kappaphycus alvarezii*; ice-ice is a literature review topic, not a prediction output. The [candidate host and region table](CANDIDATE_HOST_REGIONS.md) transcribes user-provided leads for five taxon labels and four countries, with source verification still pending. Do not treat those leads as confirmed cultivation or disease observations.

Before adding a rule, record citation/DOI, studied species/strain, geography, environmental context and depth, study design, exposure duration, effect size and statistical limits, threshold and unit if justified, evidence grade, transferability, reviewer, review date, and rule version. Keep unreviewed rules inactive.

Research starting point from the brief: `https://doi.org/10.1007/s10499-026-02486-2`. Its full taxonomy and host mapping require direct verification. The brief also flags that the cited Tawi-Tawi study did not find significant correlations for most measured environmental variables; do not turn a general association into a universal temperature rule.
