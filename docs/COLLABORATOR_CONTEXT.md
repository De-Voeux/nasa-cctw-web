## What to add

In your folder, update `README.md` and add focused Markdown files when useful. Please include:

1. Your area of responsibility and the goal of your work.
2. What you have implemented, researched, designed, or decided, with links to files, issues, PRs, and sources.
3. Current status and how to reproduce or review the work.
4. Assumptions, limitations, blockers, and decisions still needed.
5. The next steps another collaborator can pick up.

Date substantial updates. Label **verified facts**, **working decisions**, and **proposals** clearly. Cite scientific and dataset claims; do not insert guessed thresholds, results, or disease probabilities. Do not store credentials, tokens, private keys, or personal data in this repository. Coordinate shared product decisions in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) so individual notes do not silently become project requirements.
