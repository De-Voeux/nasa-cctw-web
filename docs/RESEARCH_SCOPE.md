# Research scope for the SEAmon challenge

This checklist expands the research phase. It is a work plan, not evidence that an item has been verified. Preserve the relationship **species → location → condition → environment → time → source**, including evidence level and missing information.

| Area | Questions and records to collect |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Challenge fit | Verify the exact “Be an Earth Detective” challenge brief, research question, official Resources tab, and required NASA resource. The challenge title remains unconfirmed. |
| 2. Seaweed taxa | Scientific names, accepted synonyms, red/brown/green group, characteristics, habitat, life cycle, and uses. |
| 3. Geographic distribution | Natural range versus documented cultivation, country/coastal region, record date, citation, and spatial precision. |
| 4. Disease and disturbance | Disease, syndrome, parasite, epiphyte, or environmental stress; affected host, symptoms, cause if established, place, and event history. |
| 5. Environmental needs | Temperature, salinity, light, depth, currents, nutrients, pH, water quality, and species-specific tolerance plus exposure duration where supported. |
| 6. Cultivation and impact | Methods, seasons, production, productivity, losses, economic value, and ecological role, with source and context. |
| 7. Historical data inventory | NASA and complementary products: variables, years, geography, native grid, quality, access, latency, and coastal limitations. |
| 8. Change analysis | Long-term trend, seasonality, anomaly, and tested relationships with seaweed condition or reported disease; separate association from causation. |
| 9. Prediction feasibility | Define any target, horizon, labelled training data, simple baseline, and held-out historical validation before claiming predictive performance. Environmental outlooks and biological disease predictions are distinct. |
| 10. Research database | Link species, location, condition, environment, time, and source with evidence grade, uncertainty, transferability, and data gaps. |

## Deliverables before activating risk rules

- A citation-backed taxon and cultivation-region registry.
- A product-level NASA/Copernicus/Open-Meteo data inventory with tested access.
- Reviewed environmental rules with units, duration, study context, and limitations.
- A documented historical trend/anomaly method if required by the chosen challenge.
- A prediction feasibility decision: what can be supported with data, and what remains not assessable.

See [candidate host and region leads](../research/evidence/CANDIDATE_HOST_REGIONS.md). Do not turn an unsourced table into disease probabilities or map claims.
